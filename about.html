Big funds have analysts. Small investors have a spreadsheet, a few browser tabs and not enough evenings. Yardstick gives you the analyst.

The problem

Most rental investors in the United States own fewer than ten units. They find deals on Zillow, Redfin or through an agent, and then spend an hour or more per property guessing at rent, looking up tax records, calling for an insurance quote and plugging it all into a spreadsheet they built themselves.

That process is slow, so good deals get missed. It is also easy to get wrong. A rent estimate that is $200 too high or a forgotten vacancy allowance can turn a property that looks like it cash flows into one that costs money every month.

Yardstick exists to make that first pass fast, consistent and honest, so investors spend their time on the few deals that deserve a closer look.

How it works

From listing to verdict in about two minutes.

  1. Paste a listing or address

    Yardstick reads the price, beds, baths, square footage, year built and taxes from the listing or public records.

  2. AI estimates the real numbers

    It estimates market rent from comparable rentals nearby and fills in insurance, vacancy, repairs and management based on the property and the local market. You see every comp and every assumption.

  3. Add your financing and goals

    Set your down payment, rate and target return once. Yardstick applies them to every deal you run.

  4. Get a clear verdict

    Cap rate, monthly cash flow, cash on cash return and DSCR, plus a plain English explanation of what would need to change for the deal to work.

Questions

Is Yardstick financial advice?

No. Yardstick is a screening tool that does the math and shows its assumptions. Always inspect a property and talk to a lender, agent or tax professional before you buy.

What kinds of properties does it handle?

Version one covers long term rentals: single family homes, condos and small multifamily up to four units. Short term rentals and BRRRR modeling are on the roadmap.

Can I change the assumptions?

Yes. Every number is editable, and the verdict updates instantly when you change one.

Where does the AI come in?

AI reads messy listing data, picks the most comparable rentals, estimates expenses for the specific property and writes the explanation of the result. The final math is plain, auditable formulas.
